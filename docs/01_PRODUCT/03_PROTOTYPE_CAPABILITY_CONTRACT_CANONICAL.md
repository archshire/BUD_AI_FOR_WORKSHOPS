# Prototype Capability Contract

**Status:** Canonical reconstructed edition

## Purpose

This contract separates what the hackathon prototype must genuinely do from what is simplified, future-facing, or out of scope.

The repository downloaded by judges must work beyond the exact recorded demo path.

## Core claim

The prototype is a bounded but genuine AI Partner system for real-time multilingual workshops.

It must accept dynamic human input and produce context-dependent behavior rather than replaying a scripted thesis.

## MUST PROVE DEEPLY

### 1. Learner and facilitator support
Both learner and facilitator roles must have meaningful supported flows.

### 2. At least two languages
The implemented prototype must support at least two languages in the demonstrated/tested path.

### 3. Voice and text
Voice and text are first-class supported inputs for core prototype interaction where specified.

### 4. ME — participant understanding/support
The system can:
- receive participant observations;
- evaluate substantive input against current workshop context;
- form provisional evidence-linked inference;
- calibrate/check understanding;
- provide bounded support;
- revise state after new input;
- escalate/signal where warranted.

### 5. US — peer multilingual meaning repair
The system can support peer interaction where literal translation is insufficient by preserving original, translation, and contextual interpretation and helping humans confirm/repair meaning.

### 6. THE ROOM — facilitator awareness
The facilitator can receive evidence-backed operational state/pattern synthesis and recommendations without requiring unrestricted access to private learner conversations.

### 7. Privacy boundary
Private participant–AI content is not automatically exposed to facilitators.

Where private access is supported, permission is enforced by application logic.

### 8. Language pipeline
The prototype preserves original evidence, translates at meaningful utterance boundaries, and invokes deeper reasoning when warranted rather than on every fragment.

### 9. Non-scripted intelligence
Varied inputs within the supported domain must produce context-dependent evaluation.

The prototype must not rely on hard-coded demo misunderstandings or fixed intervention timing.

## REQUIRED BEHAVIORAL SAFEGUARDS

Within prototype depth, the system should demonstrate or preserve:

- unknown/insufficient evidence rather than forced judgment;
- revisable inference;
- human correction of AI interpretation;
- productive disagreement not automatically classified as failure;
- minimum sufficient intervention;
- WAIT/NO_ACTION as valid behavior;
- privacy-aware projection;
- system self-error hypothesis for STT/translation/AI mistakes;
- facilitator authority over consequential workshop decisions.

## ARCHITECTURALLY SUPPORTED / MAY BE LIGHTER

The architecture should permit, without requiring production-depth implementation:

- advanced longitudinal trajectory;
- sophisticated multi-group inference;
- fine-grained consent scopes;
- complex intervention arbitration;
- extensive workshop co-definition UI;
- advanced accessibility/compliance coverage;
- Zoom adapter;
- Google Meet adapter;
- durable multi-workshop memory;
- multi-agent orchestration;
- local/open-source inference.

## Allowed prototype constraints

The team may:
- constrain participant count;
- constrain guaranteed languages;
- seed workshop setup;
- simplify persistence;
- use managed APIs/services;
- simplify permission UX while preserving the actual boundary;
- simulate disclosed visual scale.

## Prohibited prototype shortcuts

The team must not:
- hard-code intelligence claimed as dynamic;
- fake permission enforcement;
- present seeded recommendations as AI inference;
- claim production security/compliance;
- claim integrations that do not work;
- present arbitrary learner scores as validated measurements;
- expose private content merely because it was paraphrased.

## Success test

A judge should be able to vary participant wording/scenario within the documented domain and observe the system:
1. capture evidence;
2. reason from current workshop context;
3. produce a structured, bounded response;
4. update state from subsequent human input;
5. preserve privacy/authority boundaries.

> **Constrain the domain, not the intelligence.**
